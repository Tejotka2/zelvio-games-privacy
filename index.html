FOLD POP! Privacy Policy

Android game · com.lumipogames.foldpop

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Zelvio Games develops FOLD POP!. This policy explains how information is handled in our current Google Play release, version 0.4.1 (10), and when you visit this policy website or contact us. Privacy contact: tjslpl@gmail.com.

Information used on your device

The game stores high scores, game and cleared-block totals, unlocked themes, sound, vibration and animation preferences, and tutorial completion in its local app storage. It also uses the current board, score, combo streak, gadget usage and revival state in memory to run the game. A saved high score is not a public leaderboard. We do not require an account, name, email address or login to play.

The production game does not send this gameplay information to Zelvio Games or an analytics server. It does not collect your contacts, precise location, advertising ID, microphone recordings or camera images. There is no cloud-save service or remote database operated by us.

Advertising, SDKs and diagnostics

This production release contains no Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK or Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) SDK, serves no advertisements and does not offer ad-based REVIVE. Free restart and the existing free gadgets do not require an advertisement. We do not access an advertising identifier, use personalized advertising or operate an advertising-consent flow in this release.

The game uses the Godot engine and Android support libraries to render the game and interact with Android. We have not integrated analytics, third-party crash reporting, social-login or tracking services into this release. Optional local playtest diagnostics belong to developer/debug builds and cannot be enabled in the production release. Debug advertising tests are not part of the release described here. If a future update introduces advertising or other data processing, we will update this policy and provide applicable choices before that processing begins.

Opening this website and contacting us

The Privacy Policy button opens this public HTTPS page in your browser; it does not upload your score or save file. GitHub Pages hosts this website. When your browser requests it, GitHub processes connection and request information, such as your IP address and browser information, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Browser and network providers may also process requests according to their own practices. This site adds no analytics, tracking scripts, cookies, web fonts, account system or contact form.

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address, the content you send and any attachments. We use that information to respond to your request and address support or privacy issues, and may use the email service provider needed to receive and reply to messages. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information. Email is optional and is separate from playing the game.

Purposes and sharing

Local data is used to run the puzzle, remember preferences and retain your progress and records. Support correspondence is used to answer your request. We do not sell personal information or share gameplay data with advertisers. We do not operate a game-data server. Our hosting/email providers process information needed for those separate services; information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you clear the app's storage, uninstall the game, or your device removes the data. The app disables Android automatic app-data backup, but device manufacturers, operating-system tools or backups you make may behave differently. Clearing storage removes local records and settings and cannot be undone through us. You can normally do this under Android Settings → Apps → FOLD POP! → Storage → Clear storage; wording varies by device.

We cannot remotely retrieve or delete game records stored only on your device. For support messages or privacy requests, contact us using the address above. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations; you may request deletion, subject to those obligations. GitHub controls retention of its hosting records under its own policy. There is no game account to delete.

Security and choices

The game uses Android's app storage protections. This policy page is served over HTTPS. These measures do not guarantee absolute security, and we do not claim that local save files are specially encrypted. You may adjust sound, vibration and animation settings, clear local storage or uninstall the game. No advertising or analytics consent is requested because those services are not active in this production release.

Children and intended audience

FOLD POP! is intended for teenagers aged 13 and above and adults; it is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent personal information to our support contact, please contact us so we can address it and delete it where appropriate. A store content rating is separate from our intended audience.

Changes and contact

We may revise this policy as the game or our practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be explained through an appropriate notice. For questions, privacy choices or deletion requests concerning information you sent us, contact Zelvio Games at tjslpl@gmail.com.
