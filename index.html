FOLD POP! Privacy Policy

Android game · com.lumipogames.foldpop

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Zelvio Games develops FOLD POP! (Android package com.lumipogames.foldpop). This policy covers version 0.5.0 and later versions with the practices described here. Earlier version 0.4.1 did not include advertising. Privacy contact: tjslpl@gmail.com.

Local gameplay information

FOLD POP! requires no account or login. Scores, records, preferences, theme progress and tutorial completion are saved in local app storage. The current board, combo streak, gadget and revival state are also processed to run the game. Zelvio Games does not upload these gameplay records to a game server, public leaderboard or cloud-save service.

The game does not request contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. Optional developer playtest diagnostics are local, bounded and disabled in production. There is no separate analytics or crash-reporting integration operated by Zelvio Games.

Advertising and consent

The Android release includes Google's Mobile Ads SDK through the Poing Godot AdMob plugin and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Rewarded advertising can offer an optional REVIVE after Game Over when an ad is available. A successful reward continues the current run; you may instead restart for free. No banner or interstitial advertisements are requested by this version.

Google's advertising services can process IP-derived approximate location, ad and app interactions, performance diagnostics, and device or account-related identifiers such as the Android advertising ID and app set ID. These support advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Consent and privacy choices are handled through Google's UMP where applicable; they may affect the ads and data processing available. Google may process this information outside your country. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google advertising information. We do not send your locally saved scores or board to Google as an advertising parameter.

Before initializing or requesting advertisements, the game requests current UMP consent information and permits ads only after the SDK reports consent obtained or not required. If this check fails, ads remain unavailable. Where UMP requires it, Settings includes Ad privacy choices so you can revisit the available choices. Availability and presentation depend on your region and Google's configured messages. Rejecting personalized advertising does not necessarily prevent all advertising-related processing. Android also provides controls to reset or delete your advertising ID. Playing and restarting do not require choosing REVIVE.

Purposes and sharing

Local records support gameplay and remember your preferences. Advertising partners process data to provide and measure ads and prevent abuse; Google may share data according to its policies and applicable choices. We do not sell your saved game records or operate a remote game-data database. We may process information needed to meet legal obligations or respond to a request you send us.

This website and support

The in-game Privacy Policy link opens this public HTTPS page in your browser without sending a score or save file. GitHub Pages hosts this site and processes ordinary connection/request information, including IP addresses, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. This page adds no analytics scripts, tracking cookies, external fonts, accounts or contact forms.

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments and use our email provider to handle support and privacy requests. Sending an email is optional. Please avoid unnecessary sensitive information and never send passwords.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you clear app storage, uninstall, or your device removes them. Android automatic app-data backup is disabled; manufacturer tools or backups you make may behave differently. Android Settings → Apps → FOLD POP! → Storage normally provides a clear-storage option. This removes local records and preferences. We cannot recover or remotely delete device-only saves.

Google and GitHub determine retention and deletion for information they process under their respective policies and legal obligations. For Google privacy controls, visit Google Data & privacy. Clearing the game's local storage does not itself delete information already held by providers. We retain support correspondence only as needed to address the request and applicable legal requirements. You may request its deletion by emailing us. There is no game account to delete.

Security and privacy rights

Android app storage protections help protect local records; we do not claim specially encrypted save files or absolute security. Google states its advertising SDK transmits collected user data with TLS encryption. This policy is served over HTTPS. You may exercise available privacy choices in the game and Android settings, uninstall, or contact us about rights such as access, correction, deletion or objection where applicable. Requests concerning information held by Google should also be directed to Google's privacy controls.

Children and intended audience

FOLD POP! is intended for teenagers aged 13 and above and adults, and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it. Advertising choices and age-related protections can vary by jurisdiction; a store content rating is separate from the game's intended audience.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or our practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. Material changes will be explained through an appropriate notice. For privacy questions and requests, contact Zelvio Games at tjslpl@gmail.com.
